Media Renamer Pro vs A Better Finder Rename: which Mac renamer fits your media

A Better Finder Rename has been a Mac batch renamer since 1996. Media Renamer Pro looks up what your files actually are. Here is how they compare, with A Better Finder Rename's side taken from its own website.

A Better Finder Rename renames files from rules you build: text actions, sequence numbers, regular expressions and the metadata tags already in the file. Media Renamer Pro reads those tags too, then goes further and looks up the real movie, episode, audiobook or game title online. The table comes first.

What A Better Finder Rename is

A Better Finder Rename is a Mac batch renamer from publicspace.net. Its site lists a live preview with change highlighting, 15 categories of renaming actions, multi-step renames with filters, regular expressions, names imported from a spreadsheet, presets and droplets, and tag-based renaming from photo, music, movie, camera, lens and location metadata. Photographers get EXIF dates, sequence numbers, time zone handling and RAW+JPEG pairing.

Where Media Renamer Pro is different

  • It knows what your files are. The.Last.of.Us.S02E04.1080p.WEB-DL.mkv becomes the real show name, episode number and episode title, because Media Renamer Pro looks it up. The same goes for movies, audiobooks and game ROMs.
  • No rules to write. Pick a media type and a preset. Tokens are click-to-add, with no regex needed.
  • It runs on your iPhone and iPad too. One purchase covers all three.
  • Undo for the whole batch. The last saved rename session can be put back from its log.
  • Tags and cover art. Version 2.0 edits title, artist, album, show, year and cover art inside MP3, MP4 and MKV files.

Price

Media Renamer Pro is US$4.99, once, for Mac, iPhone and iPad. A Better Finder Rename's order page listed a single-user license at US$29.95 on November 2, 2026, with family, business and forever-upgrade options at other prices.
