Private by design

Renaming happens on your device. Apple Vision is the default photo analysis engine and processes photos on-device. Optional cloud engines (OpenAI, Gemini and Anthropic) send photos to the selected provider when you configure your own key and use that engine. Online title lookups and location lookups also need a connection. Turn on Local-Only Mode to block network requests. The app has no account, tracking, ads, or telemetry. Your first rename

Start with a copied folder of a few files. Choose the media type, review the proposed names, then apply the changes.

  1. Choose your folder. On Mac, if Grant Access appears, go up one level in the picker, select the target folder itself, then click Grant Access.
  2. Preview. Check the old and new filenames. Enable Include subfolders only if you want to scan inside nested folders.
  3. Rename, then check the result. Keep the app open and your drive connected until the batch finishes. Undo reverts the last saved session while its files are still accessible.

Before a large batch

Keep a backup. Undo history is saved after a batch finishes; it is not crash recovery. A force quit, power loss or lost network connection can leave temporary filenames or changes that need manual recovery.

Version 1.3.2 can carry companion subtitle files along with a renamed video once you turn that on in Settings. It deliberately leaves artwork and .nfo sidecars alone, because media centres look those up by exact name. It does not preserve Live Photo pairing, and an audiobook series naming token is not available yet.
