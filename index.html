Your first rename

Three steps, every time

Start with a copied folder of a few files. You will know within a minute whether it names things the way you like.

  1. Add files or a folder. Pick the media type, then add files with + or drop them in. On Mac, if Grant Access appears, select the folder itself and click Grant Access.
  2. Preview. Check the old and new names, the source of each match and its confidence. Any new name can be edited.
  3. Rename. Keep the app open and the drive connected until the batch finishes. Undo puts the last batch back, and Undo a Past Batch reaches any earlier one.

Questions

Quick answers

Is Media Renamer Pro free?

It is a one-time purchase of US $4.99 on the App Store. No subscription, no ads, no account, and one purchase covers Mac, iPad and iPhone.

Will it rename anything without asking?

No. It builds a rename plan and shows every old and new name side by side. Nothing on disk changes until you press Rename. Undo puts the last batch back, and Undo a Past Batch reaches any earlier one.

Does it work with Plex, Jellyfin and Emby?

Yes. The default TV and movie templates follow the naming those servers read, like Show S01E02 Episode Title and Movie (Year), and you can change them. The naming guide explains why.

Can it rename files on a NAS?

Yes. Mount the share in Finder (or pick it in Files on iPhone and iPad) and preview as normal. You can save it as a Watched Folder and load only the new files automatically.

Do I need API keys?

No. TVmaze, Apple Music, MusicBrainz, OpenLibrary and RAWG need nothing, and Apple Intelligence photo names are free with no key. TMDb, OMDb and IGDB can use your own free key for better results.

Can I undo a batch from last week?

Yes. Click the arrow next to Undo (touch and hold on iPhone and iPad), choose Undo a Past Batch and pick it from the list. Files that were moved, deleted or renamed again since are skipped, never overwritten. It works for any batch whose log is still in the export folder, and the app keeps the last 100.

What do Apple Intelligence photo names need?

macOS 27 or iOS 27 on a device that supports Apple Intelligence. It runs on Apple's Private Cloud Compute for free, with a daily limit. On older systems, or past the limit, the app uses Apple Vision on your device instead.
