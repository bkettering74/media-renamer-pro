Media Renamer Pro support

Something not behaving? Start here. If the answer is not below, email me and I will actually reply.

Email: mediarenamerpro@gmail.com
Include your device, your macOS or iOS version, the app version, and a couple of example filenames before and after. Most replies go out within two business days.

Community: Prefer chat? Join the Media Renamer Pro Discord to ask questions, share your naming setups and suggest features. Email still works for anything private.

Getting started

Nothing happens after I add files.

Renaming never starts on its own. Add your files, pick the media type tab, then press Preview. The app builds a plan and shows you every old and new name. Nothing on disk changes until you press Rename.

On Mac it says Grant Access and will not let me continue.

macOS only gives the app the exact folder you select. In the picker, go up one level, select the target folder itself rather than looking inside it, then click Grant Access. If you hand the app individual files instead of a folder, it can work with those files but cannot see anything else in the folder.

Do I need an API key?

No. The app works without one. TVmaze, Apple Music, MusicBrainz, OpenLibrary and RAWG need nothing, and Apple Intelligence photo names are free with no key. TMDb, OMDb and IGDB use your own free key if you add one in Settings. Optional cloud photo engines use your own Anthropic, Google, OpenAI or xAI key, stored only in your Keychain. There is no account and nothing to subscribe to.

How do I rename from Finder?

In version 1.3.2 and later, select files in Finder, right click, and choose Rename with Media Renamer Pro. The app opens with those files already loaded. If you do not see the item, open the app once first, then look under the Services submenu.

New in 2.1.5

How do I fix albums that came from a greatest hits or compilation?

Select the songs, right-click and choose Fill tags from lookup, then turn on Also replace albums from compilations, live releases and bootlegs. The preview shows every change as Album: X (was Y) before anything is written. Live albums, remixes, demos and unreleased sets are left alone, and Undo Last Tag Change puts everything back, even after you close the app.

New in 2.1.3

How do I undo a batch from earlier, not just the last one?

Click the arrow next to Undo (touch and hold Undo on iPhone and iPad) and choose Undo a Past Batch. Pick the batch, confirm, and every file goes back to the name it had before that batch. Files that were moved, deleted or renamed again since are skipped, never overwritten.

My batch is not in the Undo a Past Batch list.

The list is built from the rename logs in your export folder, so Export rollback file must be on (Settings, Export and Safety; it is on by default). Previews and logs from before 2.1.3 are hidden until you turn on Show previews and older logs. The app keeps the newest 100 logs and never removes one from the last 7 days.

Why does Undo a Past Batch ask me to pick a folder?

Apple only lets the app back into folders you have handed it. It remembers the folders from your latest batch, so an older batch somewhere else needs one pick. The picker opens in the right place; choose that folder (or any folder above it) and the whole batch goes back.

What happens if a rename gets interrupted?

If the app quits, the Mac sleeps or a drive disconnects mid-batch, the app puts those files back on its own the next time it opens. If the drive is still missing, it waits and tries again on the next launch. Nothing is left with a strange temporary name.

How do Apple Intelligence photo names work?

Pick the engine in Settings, Photos and AI. Apple Intelligence (Private Cloud) is the default where it is available: it sends each photo to Apple's Private Cloud Compute, Apple does not keep or see it, and there is no key or cost. It needs macOS 27 or iOS 27 and has a daily limit; past it, or on older systems, the app uses Apple Vision on your device. Local-Only Mode never uses Private Cloud.

Can I save a photo's description into the file?

Yes. Open the photo's info panel from the tag icon on its row and press Save description into the photo. The description is added as the photo's caption and keywords, so Photos, Plex and Finder search can find it. The picture itself is not changed.

How do I delete my face data?

Open Settings, Photos and AI, and press Delete All Face Data in the face recognition area. It removes every enrolled person from this device. Saved presets never keep a copy of your faces.

How do I share faces with family?

Open Settings, Photos and AI, and in Face Recognition choose Share People. Pick the people, confirm they agreed to be shared, and save the file. Send it by AirDrop, Messages or email. On their device, family choose Import People and open the file. Nothing goes through a server, and importing never removes or renames anyone already in their library.

Where are my API keys stored?

Only in your device's Keychain. They are never saved in settings files and are kept out of run logs, so a log you send to support never contains a key.

What does Local-Only Mode block?

Every internet connection, including lookups you start by hand, like Fix match or Find cover online. Renaming from the tags and names already in your files still works, and a local Ollama server on your own network still works. Turn it on in Settings, General, Privacy.

Can I change the app color?

Yes. Settings, General, Color has 11 to choose from. Purple is the original.

Folders mode did not rename my Movies folder.

That is on purpose. Folders mode never renames your library folders, like Movies, TV Shows or Audio Books. It renames the folders inside them.

My TV files have no episode number in the name.

Put them in their Season folder and preview again. The app takes the season from the folder and the episode number from names like "Episode 4", "Chapter 04" or "308", matches split episodes by title, and turns "Chapter 19 and 20" into a two-episode name.

New in 2.0

How do I edit tags and cover art?

Turn on Tag editor in Settings, General, Tags (it is off until you do). Then right-click a file in the list, or long-press on iPhone and iPad, and choose Edit tags. Change the fields, press Preview changes to see the before and after, then Write tags. Undo puts the file back exactly. The edit tags guide has the full steps.

I cannot find Edit tags.

The Tag editor switch in Settings, General, Tags is off by default, and the menu item only appears when it is on and the file type can be written. Tags can be written in MP3, MP4, M4V, M4A, M4B, MKV and WebM files. Other types, such as FLAC, OGG, MOV and AVI, are renamed only. Game files are not tag edited.

Does changing tags change my files?

Yes. Changing tags changes the file itself. Apps that watch files for changes (backup, sync, download or media-manager tools) may treat it as a new or changed file. Undo puts it back exactly. Try a small folder first and keep a backup of anything you cannot replace.

How do I fix a wrong match?

Click the tag icon on the row and choose Fix match, or open it from the Edit tags panel. Search by title and year (artist for music, author for audiobooks), pick the right result and press Use this match. Nothing is renamed until you apply, and your pick is remembered. See how to fix a wrong match.

How do I clear the matches it remembered?

Open Settings and look in the Tags area. It shows how many Fix match picks are remembered, with a Forget All button.

What does the confidence percentage mean?

It shows how much evidence backs a match: how closely the title matches, whether the year agrees, how many candidates there were, and for music and audiobooks whether the artist or author agrees. Hover over it to see the reason. A pick the app had to guess from many results never shows as high. See what the confidence percentage means.

How do I clean junk tags or fill empty ones?

Right-click rows in the preview and choose Clean tags, Fill tags from filename or Fill tags from lookup. Each one lists every file and what will change before anything is written, and one Undo reverses the whole job. Fill only ever writes blank tags. The clean and fill tags guide has the details.

Where do the cover pictures come from?

Music covers come from the online music database and its cover archive, movie and TV posters from TMDb and TVmaze, and audiobook covers from Google Books. You choose the picture. Media Renamer Pro embeds cover art inside the file's tags and still never renames, moves or deletes poster files. See how to find cover art online.

A tag write was refused. Why?

The writer refuses rather than risk your file, and it always gives a reason and leaves the file untouched. Common reasons are a cover that does not fit, a file with unusual or damaged structure, or a very large file over 400 MB whose tag area is full. When only the cover does not fit, the editor retries without it and says so.

The tags and the file name disagree. What now?

The app lists those files in a review popup and asks which to trust. Choose Keep filename and, with the tag editor and the repair option on, the tags are rewritten to match the name. See when the tags and the file name disagree.

New in 1.4

How do I add more files without starting over?

In version 1.4 and later, use the + button under your file list, or drag more files in. They join the list you already have instead of replacing it. The same file is never added twice, even when it sits inside a folder you already added. The − button only takes a row off the list. It never touches the file on disk.

What are Watched Folders and how do I set them up?

Watched Folders (version 1.4 and later) are the folders you rename most, saved so you can load them in one click. Open Settings, then Watched Folders, click Add Folder, pick the folder and give it a type: Movies, TV Shows, Music or Audiobooks. Then choose that media type and click the eye button under your file list. You still preview and confirm every rename. Watched folders do not run in the background. The rename only new files guide has the full walkthrough.

Why are some files missing after I load a watched folder?

That is New only, which is on by default. It skips files the app already renamed and hides files whose name already matches your naming pattern. Undo makes a file count as new again. If you changed a naming template, turn New only off for one run, or use Forget All in Settings, then Watched Folders, so everything is checked again.

A watched folder says Unavailable.

The drive or NAS share it lives on is not connected. Mount the share in Finder, or plug the drive in, and load again. The app shows Unavailable instead of an empty list so you never mistake a missing drive for nothing new.

Where did the Save button in Settings go?

In version 1.4 Settings save automatically as you change them. There is no Save button any more. Settings now has a sidebar, and each media type page is grouped into Naming templates and Data source.

How do I fit more rows on screen?

Turn on the Experienced layout in Settings, then General, then Layout. It hides the guidance, tightens the spacing and moves Preview, Rename and Undo to a bar at the bottom. Switch back to Guided any time.

Results and safety

Can I undo a rename?

Yes. Press Undo to reverse the last batch. To reverse an earlier one, click the arrow next to Undo (touch and hold on iPhone and iPad), choose Undo a Past Batch and pick it from the list. If a batch is cut off by a force quit, power loss or a dropped network drive, the app puts those files back on its own the next time it opens. Keep a backup before a large batch.

It renamed my file to something with _1 on the end.

That is collision protection, not a bug. Two files in the batch resolved to the same final name, so the second one was given a suffix instead of overwriting the first. The app never overwrites a file.

It picked the wrong movie or episode.

Online lookups match on the text in the filename, and scene releases are often ambiguous. In 2.0, click the tag icon on the row and choose Fix match to pick the right one, and your pick is remembered. You can also edit the new name in the Preview table before pressing Rename. If a whole batch is matching badly, try a different provider in Settings, or turn on Local-Only Mode to use only what is inside the files.

Does it work on a NAS or SMB share?

Yes. Mount the share in Finder and point the app at it. Preview first, and keep the share mounted until the batch finishes. Network renames are slower. If the connection drops mid-batch, the app puts those files back the next time it opens with the share mounted.

Will my subtitles follow the video?

In version 1.3.2 and later, yes, if you turn on Carry subtitle files with renamed media in Settings under Exclusions. A renamed video takes its matching subtitle files with it and keeps language and forced tags, so Plex and Jellyfin still pair them. Artwork and .nfo sidecars are deliberately left alone, because media centres look those up by exact name and renaming them breaks the library.

Privacy, purchases and contact

What happens to my photos and face data?

Renaming happens on your device. Photo descriptions come from Apple Intelligence, on your device or on Apple's Private Cloud Compute, where Apple does not keep or see your photos. Face Library profiles are stored on your device and never uploaded, saved presets never carry a copy of them, and Delete All Face Data in Settings removes them all. Optional cloud photo engines send the photo, never face crops, to the provider whose key you added. Turn on Local-Only Mode to block every internet connection. There is no account, tracking, ads or telemetry. Full details are in the privacy policy linked below.

I want a refund.

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds go through Apple rather than through me. Use reportaproblem.apple.com with the Apple Account you bought it with. If something is broken, email me first. I would rather fix it.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email mediarenamerpro@gmail.com with what you expected, what happened, and example filenames. Feature requests are welcome and genuinely do get built. Several shipped features started as one user's email.

How do I contact support?

Email mediarenamerpro@gmail.com. Include your device, your macOS or iOS version, the app version from the App Store, and a few example filenames before and after. Most replies go out within two business days.

Before a large batch

Keep a backup. Preview the whole plan first. Undo history is written after a batch finishes, so it covers a completed rename, not a crash. If you are renaming across a network share, keep the share mounted and the Mac awake until it is done.

Privacy and data

The app has no account, no tracking, no ads and no telemetry. Renaming and photo analysis happen on your device by default. Read the full app privacy policy, or the website privacy notice.
