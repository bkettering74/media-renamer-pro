Release notes

What changed in each version, in plain words.

Version 2.1.5: fix albums from greatest hits

Fix albums that came from greatest hits and bootlegs, smarter TV episode matching, and faster music lookups.

New

  • Fill tags from lookup (music) can replace an album that came from a greatest hits, compilation, live release or bootleg with the album the song first came out on, and its year. Turn on the switch in the panel. Every change shows as "Album: X (was Y)" before anything is written. Live albums, remixes, demos and unreleased sets are left alone.
  • Mac: when you rename picked files, the app asks once for permission to their folder, before renaming, and remembers it. Settings, Export & Safety lists the folders you allowed, with Forget.
  • Help links to the naming guides for Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, Audiobookshelf and more.

Improved

  • TV: episode files with only a number, like "One Piece - 0892 - Title" or "Episode 1 Rebirth", are matched to the right season and episode when the episode title agrees.
  • TV: leftover "(1)" endings are removed, while real two-part episodes keep their part numbers.
  • TV: files named "Show - S01E02 (Title)" move to the standard layout, and words like "Fox" stay in titles.
  • Music: lookups are faster, and Undo Last Tag Change works even after you close the app.
  • Music: a bootleg, a live session or a placeholder like "3 CD Set" is never used to fill a blank album.
  • The first online lookup of a batch starts faster for audiobooks, movies and TV.

Privacy

  • The debug log no longer contains part of your OMDb key.

Version 2.1.4: share faces with family

Share your Face Library with family, a guided face scan, cleaner song names and a round of iPhone fixes.

New

  • Share People: in Settings, Photos and AI, Face Recognition, pick the people to share and confirm they agreed. The app saves a small file you send yourself by AirDrop, Messages or email. Nothing goes through a server.
  • Import People: open a shared file to add those people to your own Face Library. You confirm first, and nobody you already have is removed or renamed.
  • Guided face scan: Scan with Camera walks you through five head angles (straight, left, right, chin up, chin down) in about 15 seconds and keeps only sharp frames.

Improved

  • Music: upload junk like (Official Audio), (Official Video), (Lyrics), [HD] and "- YouTube" is taken out of song names and title tags. Real labels like Live, Remix, Remaster and Acoustic stay.
  • Mac: if macOS blocks a rename right after an Undo, the app asks for the folder once and tries again.
  • iPhone: Edit tags, Help, Fix match and Share People now fit the screen, the info panel is easier to read, and dimmed buttons look dimmed in dark mode.
  • Under the hood: every online lookup service now has its own code, so fixes for one service land faster. Results are the same as before.

Version 2.1.3: undo any past batch

Undo any past batch, plus safer renames, tighter privacy and a few iPhone fixes.

New

  • Undo a Past Batch: put back any earlier batch, not just the last one. Click the arrow next to Undo (touch and hold on iPhone and iPad), choose Undo a Past Batch, and pick it from the list. Previews and older logs are one switch away.
  • If the files are in a folder the app can no longer reach, it asks for that folder once, then puts the whole batch back.

Safety

  • If a rename gets interrupted (the app quits, the Mac sleeps, or a drive disconnects), your files are put back on their own next time. Nothing is left with a strange temporary name.
  • Undo will never replace a file that has since taken an old name.
  • Names longer than the file system allows are skipped with a clear reason instead of failing partway.

Privacy

  • API keys for photo naming now live only in your Keychain, and are kept out of run logs.
  • Local-Only Mode now blocks every internet connection, including lookups you start by hand. A local Ollama server still works.
  • New "Delete All Face Data" button in Settings, under Photos and Face Recognition. Saved presets no longer keep a copy of your faces.

Fixes

  • iPhone: the watched folders button now shows in portrait, next to Settings.
  • Invisible characters from online sources are removed from new names.
  • Fixed short pauses while reading photo details.

Version 2.1.1: smarter TV episode matching

TV shows

  • Episode numbers now come from the Season folder when a file has none, like "Episode 4", "Chapter 04", "Happy Days - 3-15" or "Teen Titans Go! 308".
  • Split episodes (S01E05a, S01E05b) are matched by title to their real episode numbers, so Emby, Plex and Jellyfin list them in the right order.
  • Two episodes in one file, like "Chapter 19 and 20", become S02E19-E20.
  • A release tag repeated on every file in a folder no longer ends up as the episode title.
  • Show names keep their official styling (VisionQuest, MobLand) without the all-caps some listings use.
  • Collection folders no longer pick up a guessed year from another show with the same name.

Fixes

  • Run logs now list the app version and key settings, so support can help faster.

Version 2.1: Apple Intelligence photo names

Free Apple Intelligence photo names, smarter music and audiobook lookups, and a fresh coat of paint. The photo naming guide has the details.

Photos

  • Apple Intelligence (Private Cloud) describes your photos on Apple's Private Cloud Compute. Free, no account, no API key. It is the new default where your device supports it.
  • Save a photo's description right into the file so it stays searchable.
  • Shorter, cleaner names, six words max.
  • Old scans are read at full detail, so handwritten captions come through.
  • Pick any image model from Anthropic, Google, OpenAI or xAI.
  • Local-Only Mode keeps photos on your device and uses the on-device model.

Music

  • Apple Music lookup first for album, year and cover art.
  • Better matches for duets, featured artists, compilations and band names. Tribute and karaoke acts are skipped.
  • Tags fill in even on MP3s that had no tags at all.

Audiobooks

  • One lookup per book, so every chapter gets the same title and cover.
  • Numbers inside titles stay put, like 2001: A Space Odyssey and Revolt in 2100.

Everywhere

  • Pick an app color in Settings. 11 to choose from.
  • Edit tags opens in its own window you can move and resize (Mac).
  • Folders mode never renames your library folders like Movies, TV Shows and Audio Books.
  • A Stop button on Clean, Fill and Lookup.
  • Dozens of naming fixes from real-world libraries.

Version 2.0: edit tags, fix matches

How-to guides for everything below: edit tags and cover art, find cover art online, fix a wrong match, clean and fill tags, the confidence percentage and five workflows.

Edit tags

  • Open the tag icon on a row to see and edit the title, artist, album, show, year and cover art.
  • Preview exactly what will be written before anything is saved. Undo puts the file back exactly.
  • Clean tags wipes junk tags. Fill from filename and Fill from lookup add the missing ones. Blank fields only, and lookups fill confident matches only.
  • Works on MP3, MP4 and MKV files.
  • Find cover online shows the pictures, you pick one, and it embeds cover art inside the file's tags.

Changing tags changes the file itself. Apps that watch files for changes (backup, sync, download or media-manager tools) may treat it as a new or changed file. Undo puts it back exactly.

Fix match

  • Wrong movie, show, song or book? Pick the right one yourself. It remembers your choice.
  • The information panel opens from the tag icon on a row.

Confidence

  • The percentage on each row is now worked out from real evidence. Hover to see the reason.

Better names and speed

  • Keeps the year in your file name when a title is ambiguous.
  • TV episode titles use proper title case.
  • Real colons in tags, look-alike colons in file names.
  • Skips the hidden "._" files that Macs leave on network drives.
  • Faster on big music folders and repeat runs.
  • Watched folders: the eye button only loads the type you are on.

Version 1.4: the workflow update

  • New + and − buttons under your file list. Add more files or folders any time without starting over. − only takes a row off the list. Your files are never touched.
  • Watched Folders: save the folders you rename most and load them with the eye button. New only skips files you already renamed.
  • Experienced layout: hides the guidance, tightens the spacing and moves Preview, Rename and Undo to a bar at the bottom.
  • A new Settings with a sidebar. Changes save automatically.
  • The Show Sidebar button no longer shows up blank.

Thanks to the customer who mocked up these ideas.
