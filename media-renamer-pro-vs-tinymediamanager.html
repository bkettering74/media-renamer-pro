Media Renamer Pro vs tinyMediaManager: renaming on Mac, iPhone and iPad

tinyMediaManager is a popular free media manager for movies and TV. Media Renamer Pro is a renamer for every kind of file in your library. Here is how they compare, with tinyMediaManager's side taken from its own website.

These two tools start from different places. tinyMediaManager builds a database of your movies and shows and writes NFO files and artwork for your media center. Media Renamer Pro reads what is inside each file, looks up the real title, and renames it, across far more media types. The table comes first.

What tinyMediaManager is

tinyMediaManager is a media manager written in Java/Swing for Windows, Linux and macOS. It scans your movie and TV folders into a database, scrapes metadata, downloads artwork and trailers, writes NFO files for Kodi, Plex, Emby and Jellyfin, manages movie sets, and renames files and folders. The free version includes the TMDB scraper. PRO adds the other scrapers, subtitle downloads, Trakt.tv and external tool integration.

Where Media Renamer Pro is different

  • More than movies and TV. Photos, music, audiobooks and game ROMs get the same preview-and-rename treatment, each with its own lookups.
  • A native Apple app, on every Apple screen. Built for Mac, iPhone and iPad with one purchase. No Java, no database to build first.
  • Undo. A whole rename session can be undone from its saved log.
  • Your tags stay in your files. Edits and cover art are written inside MP3, MP4 and MKV files, so they travel with the file. Media Renamer Pro leaves NFO and poster files alone on purpose, because media servers find them by exact filename and a stale NFO can lock in a wrong match.
  • It shows its work. A confidence percentage on every row, with the reason on hover, and a review step when tags and filename disagree.

Price

Media Renamer Pro is US$4.99, once, for Mac, iPhone and iPad. tinyMediaManager has a free version, and its PRO license is 12€ for one year, as listed on its purchase page on November 2, 2026.
