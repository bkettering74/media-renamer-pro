Website privacy

Last updated September 9, 2026.

The app and the website are separate

Media Renamer Pro itself contains no analytics, advertising SDK, telemetry, or developer account system. Its app privacy details remain available on the Support and Privacy page.

Optional website analytics

This website offers optional Google Analytics 4. The analytics script does not load unless you choose Allow analytics.

If allowed, Google Analytics may record the pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser information, referring website, and clicks on links leading to the App Store. It does not receive your media filenames, app activity, email address, or anything stored inside Media Renamer Pro.

Advertising personalization and Google Signals are disabled for this property. Optional account-level data sharing with Google is also disabled.

Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage under mrp_analytics_consent. Choosing No thanks prevents the Google Analytics tag from loading.
